# Route 13

> *"Nobody on Route 13 sees your face. They see your plate."*

A chill road-trip roguelite for Roblox, inspired by *Road 96* and *a dusty trip*.

It's one night on a 100-mile desert highway run by the Mafia, the Yakuza, the Triad and the Feds. You have a car, a full tank, and a glovebox full of other people's license plates. Everyone on the road reads your plate and treats you like whoever it belongs to. You can swap it whenever you're stopped, as long as nobody sees you do it.

Get to the sea at Mile 100 by sunrise.
